# Global country-depth approval table: 27 October 2026

All 146 candidates are DRAFT; designated human approval is pending for every country. No production release or approval has changed.

## Method and coverage

The existing 84 banks receive depth extensions and 62 remaining FY27 IBRD borrowers receive new banks. The [scope roster](../research/20261026_global_depth/TARGET_COUNTRIES.json) follows the [World Bank lending groups](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/906519-world-bank-country-and-lending-groups); eligibility does not classify a country as FCV.

Additions comprise 1,716 evidence records, including 1,037 named local records. All 146 countries have at least one new named local record. The review adds 58 mediated pathways in 54 countries, of which 18 in 18 countries are reverse or bidirectional. These are selected source-supported examples and conditional mechanisms, not uniform geographic coverage or national causal estimates.

"Local E" counts new evidence records tagged to named administrative areas, sites or livelihood zones, not distinct places. "Reverse P" is a subset of new pathways. "Total E/P" includes inherited evidence and pathways; zero new pathways does not erase inherited pathways or establish absence of risk. Each country link opens its complete dossier, source locators and gaps. Counts are bound to the [review manifest](20261027_global-depth-review-manifest.json).

## Approval priorities

- Check all 351 low-confidence new records and the source-access audits before approval. Metadata-only leads and blocked reports do not support substantive claims.
- Venezuela's user-uploaded climate-report mirror requires official-source verification. Those low-confidence summaries remain test drafts; disputed-territory implications were removed.
- The restricted Uzbekistan atlas supplies no substantive bank evidence. Sources with uncertain reuse terms retain that uncertainty; no source PDFs are committed.
- Date-sensitive food-security forecasts remain dated outlooks. Older household surveys, administrative boundaries and loss estimates must not become current local rankings. Viet Nam's geographic labels refer to the source-era provinces.
- Water-use disputes, GBV/trafficking risk, violent conflict and institutional constraints remain distinct. Migration, poverty, refugee identity, nonviolent protest and human-wildlife conflict do not automatically establish climate-conflict pathways.
- Inherited deadlines were not extended. Burkina Faso, Haiti, Papua New Guinea, Ukraine and Yemen retain 10 November 2026 deadlines; South Sudan retains 31 January 2027.
