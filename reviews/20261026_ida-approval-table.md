S/E/P means source records / evidence records / conditional pathways. Local detail lists selected places or livelihood zones mentioned by sources, not complete district coverage or a vulnerability ranking. A pathway is a source-supported conditional mechanism recorded as analytical inference, not a causal effect estimate. A missing pathway is an evidence gap.

## Approval boundary

Approval must identify the country rows and the hashes in `20261026_ida-approval-manifest.json`. Changed claims, sources or dossiers require another review. Country review fields and all evidence/profile/pathway statuses remain draft, with no human reviewer or approval date.

Before activation, recheck current-context claims and any expired record deadlines, create approved versioned copies using the existing workflow, build and validate a release, then obtain approval for the app pin change. Merge and deployment remain separate actions. Do not extend the existing November 2026 deadlines silently.
