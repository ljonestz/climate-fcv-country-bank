## Method and limits

The review assessed all 461 original evidence records and 141 pathways, using the original 250-entry source register. Supporting primary passages were checked for statement scope, dates, scenarios, locators, uncertainty and reuse notices. Direct PDFs/HTML, official continuous indexed passages and identified primary-study copies are distinguished in each country review. Unused metadata entries and inaccessible publications were not presented as fully verified sources. This is passage-level review, not cover-to-cover verification of every publication or an independent data reanalysis.

RETAIN means no mandatory record-level edit. REVISE includes corrected dates, horizons, geography, locators and causal wording, not necessarily a false underlying finding. DEFER excludes the item from this candidate until the documented gap is resolved. Source-register and profile corrections can also accompany a retained record. Neither a passed validator nor a RETAIN decision constitutes human approval.

Profiles were checked against surviving records. Supported local detail was preserved or narrowed, unsupported dependencies removed, and pathway roles matched to the actual evidence. Pure access-gap notes may have no surviving evidence ID; unrelated IDs were not added to satisfy a check. Current and historical observations, expired projections, planned capacity, reported outputs and evaluated outcomes remain distinct.

## Coverage accepted for this phase

These banks combine national climate and institutional baselines with selected subnational cases and pathways. They do not provide comprehensive district coverage. Local survey findings, conflict cases and programme examples must retain their locations, periods and methodological limits rather than becoming national causal claims. Current fine-scale exposure and evaluated resilience/peace outcomes remain unevenly covered.

Historical and seasonal evidence was dated rather than automatically refreshed. In particular, the Somalia FAO claims refer to the inspected 11 March 2026 edition; the URL can roll and is not a permanent archive. Some expired projections retain a future-horizon enum alongside historical solely because the existing schema requires projected records to have a future tag; their actual reference periods and expired status govern interpretation. No schema or importer code was changed to hide that limitation.
