Candidate version: `2026.10.26`

Decision status: recommendations only; designated human approval remains pending.

## Method and decision rule

Every evidence and pathway record in the 2026.08 candidate was assessed against the cited publisher document or page and locator. `RETAIN` means the dated, scoped claim and uncertainty are supported; `REVISE` means an explicit field correction was applied; `DEFER` means the claim-level passage could not be inspected or remaining support was insufficient. Catalogues, snippets and access errors were not verification. Historical observations and expired projections were not extended to 26 October 2026.

## Source access, reuse and limitations

CAF-SRC-009 and CAF-SRC-010 returned UNHCR rate limits; CAF-SRC-013 returned publisher access denial. Claim-level passages were not replaced by snippets.

All URLs were attempted on 26 October 2026. Accessible items were read from publisher PDFs or HTML; official indexed continuous text was used only where stated. Public access was not treated as an open licence: existing `unknown` statuses remain unknown, and no source PDF is committed. No review date or validity period was extended.
