# Source review: West Bank and Gaza

Review date: 26 October 2026. Candidate version: `2026.10.26`.

Recommendations and corrected drafts only. Designated human approval remains pending; no release or deployment is authorized by this review.

## Method and limits

Compared all 28 evidence records and eight pathways with relevant continuous primary passages, then checked profile synthesis against surviving records. Official PDF/HTML passages were inspected for the CCDR, NDC, PLOS review, IEG review, economic update, OCHA, UNICEF and IPC. The two older articles were inspected as complete primary-study texts in an author-uploaded rendering and a university journal archive. The RDNA was checked through its continuous official UN executive-summary extract indexed under the UN URL; its PDF could not be fetched. This was passage-level review, not a claim of cover-to-cover verification of every publication or an independent data reanalysis.

RETAIN means no mandatory record edit; REVISE means content, scope, horizon or locator correction; DEFER excludes the unsupported item from the new candidate. A failed fetch does not establish that a claim is false. Historical material is not refreshed into a present observation merely by this review.
