## Climate pressures and exposure

- The World Bank summary projects economic losses could reach 17% of GDP by 2050 without reforms, diversification and climate action; it also reports health costs rising from US$92 million in 2010 to US$260 million by 2050. Scenario: Without reforms, diversification and climate action, as stated in the summary; underlying model assumptions are not stated there. [COG-E-001]
- The NDC describes repeated low river-water levels and identifies coastal flooding, erosion, submersion and salinization among risks to coastal areas; it says sea-level rise was not yet perceptible in the assessment. [COG-E-004]
- The IMF report says major floods have become more recurrent, affecting the river corridor and Brazzaville and Pointe-Noire; the Congo and Oubangui reached record levels during the 2023–2024 rainy season. [COG-E-005]
- Source dated 2025-12 reports: At Pointe-Noire's Tchikobo-Songolo corridor, project screening found high susceptibility to adjacent coastal floods and fluvial flooding intensified by rainfall runoff. [COG-E-107]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- The NDC assesses hydropower as moderately exposed to climate hazards, particularly uneven rainfall and drought, because generation depends on surface-water availability and river flows. [COG-E-003]
- For December 2023 floods across nine departments, initial government estimates as of January 2024 counted 336,560 people affected and 23 deaths; homes, schools, health centers, water and sanitation were disrupted, and farming and fishing halted. [COG-E-006]
- Using EM-DAT data from 2000, the IMF chapter reports a flood about every two and a half years and an average of approximately 92,000 people affected per flood event. [COG-E-007]
- Source dated 2025-12 reports: In Brazzaville's Tsieme catchment, runoff-fed active gullies at Ngambio and Itsali in Mfilou threaten nearby homes and infrastructure; at Ngambio, erosion continues despite existing channels. [COG-E-101]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- The World Bank summary lists climate-ready health and education services, disease surveillance, climate-smart agriculture and forest management among proposed priorities; these are recommendations, not verified delivery. [COG-E-002]
- Source dated 2025-12 reports: November 2025 demonstrations observed sediment trapped by bamboo check-dams, considered locally replicable; site screening weighs security, institutions and social tensions. [COG-E-103]

## Institutions and delivery systems

- Source dated 2025-12 reports: Exposed informal settlements, undersized drainage and catchment degradation amplify flood and erosion impacts; where land tenure is unclear, the report advises conflict-sensitive consultation and due diligence before works. [COG-E-102]
