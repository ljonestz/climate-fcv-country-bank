## Climate pressures and exposure

- The release reports 2015–16 drought affected 18 of Cambodia’s 25 provinces and over 2.5 million people. [KHM-E-001]
- The release says September 2022 rainfall flooded 14 provinces, affecting about 85,000 households; landslides prompted 5,000 household evacuations. [KHM-E-003]
- NDC 3.0 reports major floods recur about every five years, severe agricultural drought has a 4% median annual probability, and the southern plains face severe drought roughly every five to six years. [KHM-E-005]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- The release estimates 2019 El Niño drought-related rice-production losses at US$100 million. [KHM-E-002]
- The CCDR projects worsening flood risk and heat stress; without proper adaptation and mitigation, climate costs could reach 9% of GDP by 2050 and poverty rise 6 percentage points by 2040. [KHM-E-004]
- The NDC identifies low-income households along the Mekong, Tonle Sap Basin, and northwest Cambodia as disproportionately exposed to floods, drought, and heat, with risks to agricultural livelihoods. [KHM-E-006]
