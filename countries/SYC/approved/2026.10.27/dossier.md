## Climate pressures and exposure

- Source dated 2026-03 reports: CCDR locates 90% of people and key infrastructure in coastal areas; heavy rain with king tides drives floods, soil/beach erosion and landslides. [SYC-E-101]
- Source dated 2026-03 reports: About 48% of roads on Mahé, Praslin and La Digue are exposed to coastal erosion/flooding; a 2023 SLTA study flags Bel Ombre, Port Glaud, Au Cap and L’Amitié Road. [SYC-E-103]
- Source dated 2023-12 reports: At four Mahé zones and Praslin/La Digue, TNC projects little mean-annual rainfall change but 4–8 additional extreme wet days per year from a two-day historical average. Scenario: ALADIN projections under SSP126, SSP245 and SSP585 through 2100. [SYC-E-105]
- Source dated 2023-12 reports: TNC identifies Anse Kerlan (north-west Praslin) and Anse Royale (south-east Mahé) among eroded, vulnerable beaches, citing steep reef bathymetry that lets waves break close to shore. [SYC-E-106]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- The CCDR's regional indicator shows different hazard-risk patterns in Praslin/La Digue and the West region under moderate and rare severe events. Its table and prose use different population/household labels, so precise percentages are not carried forward. [SYC-E-102]
- Source dated 2023-12 reports: TNC relays a literature-based hypothesis that climate change may shift purse-seine tuna zones beyond Seychelles’ EEZ, reducing Port Victoria’s attractiveness and affecting fishing livelihoods and fish supply. Scenario: Emissions pathway not specified by cited hypothesis. [SYC-E-107]
- Source dated 2023-12 reports: TNC says most primary-care facilities are low-lying and often flooded during heavy rain, interrupting services; limited land constrains relocation and shoreline setbacks. [SYC-E-108]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- The CCDR describes emergency social assistance following the December 2023 disaster and gaps in hazard targeting, geospatial beneficiary data and evacuation planning for Home Care clients with mobility constraints. [SYC-E-104]
- Source dated 2024-05-22 reports: UNDP’s 2024–28 programme plans to embed ecosystem-service valuation in sector plans to build climate resilience and early warning, manage resources, and support alternative livelihoods for women in fisher communities and at-risk or underemployed youth. [SYC-E-112]

## Institutions and delivery systems

- Source dated 2026-07-10 reports: UNDP, the Government and China CIDCA handed 35 tablets to DRDM for more efficient real-time disaster and climate-hazard data collection and sharing. [SYC-E-109]
- Source dated 2026-07-10 reports: TIAEWS aims to implement an integrated disaster-information system, improve real-time warnings and crisis communication, and connect communities with first responders. [SYC-E-110]
- Source dated not stated reports: The department says the Disaster Risk Management Division became a Principal-Secretary-led DRDM under the Vice-President in December 2025, aiming to strengthen governance and operational coordination. [SYC-E-111]
