## Climate pressures and exposure

- The World Bank summary says sea-level rise threatens coastlines on which much of Cabo Verde’s tourism depends. [CPV-E-001]
- The summary identifies warming oceans and overfishing as threats to fisheries, which accounted for 72% of exports in 2021. [CPV-E-003]
- Without action, the CCDR summary projects GDP could be up to 3.6% smaller in 2050 and tourism revenue up to 10% lower. [CPV-E-004]
- The NDC reports about 0.14°C warming per decade (roughly 0.7°C over 50 years); rainfall is highly variable and has no statistically significant long-term national trend. [CPV-E-005]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- The summary identifies drought and saline intrusion as threats to agriculture, income and food security in rural communities of this water-scarce country. [CPV-E-002]
- The NDC says rural and peri-urban women in agriculture, water management and care face higher workloads and reduced income during prolonged drought and resource scarcity, while remaining underrepresented in climate governance. [CPV-E-006]
- The NDC identifies youth risks linked to limited jobs, education and decision participation, and children’s exposure to climate-related health risks, food insecurity and schooling disruption. [CPV-E-007]
