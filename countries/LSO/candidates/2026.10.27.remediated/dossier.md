## Climate pressures and exposure

- The profile reports 2018/19 yields fell over 60% versus 2018 (maize 78%, wheat 61%, sorghum 93%). [LSO-E-001]
- The assessment summary says all climate models considered project rising temperatures for Lesotho, while precipitation projections differ, complicating water-availability estimates through 2050. Scenario: The inspected summary does not specify it. [LSO-E-005]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- The profile map ranks 42% of agricultural areas in five named districts among Lesotho's most drought-prone. [LSO-E-003]
- Source dated 2016-07-25 reports: The report says children collecting water or food at community points, and children walking longer distances during drought, faced increased danger and injury risk. [LSO-E-105]
- Source dated 2026-03-02 reports: The UN identifies Mafeteng, Mohale's Hoek and Quthing in the Southern Lowlands as facing recurrent drought and land degradation that undermine farming; Thaba-Tseka in the Highlands faces drought, erosion and limited services alongside high poverty and chronic malnutrition. [LSO-E-107]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- The assessment identifies water infrastructure and adaptation investments as options to strengthen domestic and industrial water security, irrigation and food production, and regional water transfers. Scenario: The inspected summary does not specify it. [LSO-E-006]
- Source dated 2026-03-02 reports: Phase II plans better seasonal forecasts, gender-responsive last-mile climate services, anticipatory action, ecosystem rehabilitation, livelihood diversification and improved market links; it prioritizes women-headed households, youth, persons with disabilities and remote rural communities. [LSO-E-109]

## Institutions and delivery systems

- The profile rates early warning low and notes weak institutional coordination and no protocol. [LSO-E-004]
- Source dated not stated reports: The UN reported 2025 support for restoration in degraded areas of Mafeteng, Mohale's Hoek and Quthing, including agroforestry, 400 indigenous trees and a check dam; it also reported drought anticipatory-action protocols and improved early-warning procedures for the DMA. [LSO-E-106]
- Source dated 2026-03-02 reports: The UN reports Phase I strengthened national climate-information systems, supported climate-smart agriculture and community risk knowledge; Phase II is funded for US$10 million, implemented by WFP and executed by two ministries. [LSO-E-108]

## Climate-FCV pathways

- Drought-related water journeys can increase protection risks for women and girls. [LSO-P-001; LSO-E-002]
  - Documented impact: Rural water sources dry up.
  - FCV mediator: Longer water-collection journeys for women and girls.
  - Possible consequence: Heightened exposure to abuse and violence.
