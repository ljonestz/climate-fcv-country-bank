## Climate pressures and exposure

- The profile reports 2018/19 yields fell over 60% versus 2018 (maize 78%, wheat 61%, sorghum 93%). [LSO-E-001]
- The assessment summary says all climate models considered project rising temperatures for Lesotho, while precipitation projections differ, complicating water-availability estimates through 2050. [LSO-E-005]

## Climate-FCV pathways

- Drought-related water journeys can increase protection risks for women and girls. [LSO-P-001; LSO-E-002]
  - Documented impact: Rural water sources dry up.
  - FCV mediator: Longer water-collection journeys for women and girls.
  - Possible consequence: Heightened exposure to abuse and violence.
  - Link evidence: {'pressure': ['LSO-E-002'], 'impact': ['LSO-E-002'], 'mediator': ['LSO-E-002'], 'consequence': ['LSO-E-002']}
  - Alternative explanations: ['Baseline gender inequalities and protection conditions also shape risk.']
  - Uncertainty: Historical protection-risk account; no measured violence effect or event attribution.
