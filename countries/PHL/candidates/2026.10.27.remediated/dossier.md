## Climate pressures and exposure

- Source dated 2021 reports: The 1991–2020 rainfall baseline varied from about 960 mm in southeast Mindanao to over 4,050 mm in central Luzon; periodic drought is linked to ENSO. [PHL-E-101]
- Source dated 2021 reports: Under RCP8.5, the profile projects about 3.4°C warming over Luzon and Mindanao in 2080–2099 using downscaled data; smaller-island and rainfall estimates remain uncertain. Scenario: RCP8.5; downscaled KNMI Climate Explorer projection for 2080–2099 [PHL-E-102]
- Source dated not stated reports: Reported hazards vary: erosion/landslide across six provinces; flood in Lanao del Sur and Maguindanao provinces; surge in Basilan/Tawi-Tawi; drought in Sulu/Maguindanao del Norte. [PHL-E-105]
- Source dated 2026-04-15 reports: The UN reports 23 cyclones in 2025, water insecurity and uneven local preparedness. [PHL-E-112]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- Source dated 2021 reports: The profile says poorer farmers and communities are less able to afford water storage, irrigation and adaptation technology, while poorer groups are often more exposed to floods and mudflows. [PHL-E-103]
- Source dated not stated reports: DA rates most priority-crop areas across the six BARMM provinces as highly climate-vulnerable. [PHL-E-104]
- Source dated not stated reports: Under RCP8.5, rice/coconut conditions worsen in five provinces, banana in Lanao del Sur, and coconut in Basilan; rice suitability improves in Basilan. Scenario: RCP8.5; projection period unstated [PHL-E-106]
- Source dated not stated reports: The World Bank summary says that without action climate change could cut GDP by up to 13.6% by 2040, with poorest households most affected; effects vary within and across regions. Scenario: The landing-page summary says 'if nothing is done' but does not specify a model scenario. [PHL-E-118]

## Climate-FCV pathways

- The 2023 Tandubas workshop account links coastal livelihood/water pressures and inland movement with ancestral-site disputes; it is a low-confidence, source-stated local mechanism, not a measured climate effect on violence. [PHL-P-101; PHL-E-108]
  - Documented impact: The Tandubas workshop case describes salinity/freshwater losses, affected fishing/seaweed livelihoods and movement inland.
  - FCV mediator: Inland settlement encountering ancestral sites and contested land/cultural rights.
  - Possible consequence: Participants linked this to disputes, cultural conflict and crime/violence; no incidence estimate or causal identification.
