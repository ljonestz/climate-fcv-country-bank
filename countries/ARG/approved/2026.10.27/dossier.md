## Climate pressures and exposure

- The December 2020 NDC describes historical 1960–2010 rainfall-linked expansion of permanent water bodies in parts of central/eastern Argentina, alongside high rainfall variability. [ARG-E-101]
- Source dated 2020-12 reports: For end-century RCP8.5, it projects 10–20% less rainfall in western Patagonia and the Mendoza Andes, with similar increases in central/eastern Argentina. Scenario: RCP8.5; end-century. [ARG-E-103]
- Source dated 2025-04 reports: INA's April report records 16 reported deaths and 1,000+ evacuees; estimated depths reached 2.0 m in Barrio Napostá and 0.8 m in Ingeniero White. [ARG-E-106]
- Source dated 2025-04 reports: Video analysis at Canal Maldonado/Agustín Álvarez estimated surface flows of 3.5–4.5 m/s; an impact-based estimate approached 5 m/s. [ARG-E-107]
- Source dated 2026-10-02 reports: Mendoza's DGI forecasts above-average 2026–2027 runoff for the Mendoza (129%), Tunuyán (117%), Diamante (112%) and Grande (111%) rivers, and near-average Atuel and Malargüe flows. Scenario: Provincial seasonal runoff forecast for 2026–2027 relative to mean flow; not an emissions scenario. [ARG-E-117]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- Source dated 2020-12 reports: NDC identifies low-income Indigenous populations as exposed due to ecosystem dependence, fragile livelihoods, limited voice and territorial displacement. [ARG-E-104]
- Source dated 2020-12 reports: NDC expects climate impacts to increase internal migration in search of better livelihoods. Scenario: No specific emissions scenario or migration horizon is given. [ARG-E-105]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- Source dated not stated reports: Argentina's 2025–2029 plan sets priorities for risk understanding, SINAGIR coordination, coordinated provincial and municipal preparedness, and resilient recovery. [ARG-E-115]

## Institutions and delivery systems

- Source dated 2025-04 reports: INA shared a preliminary 2D model of four Bahía Blanca watersheds, calibrated with public-video flood marks. [ARG-E-108]
