- The HSE article was used at abstract level only; its broad southwest/northeast/southeast patterns do not name federal subjects and its heuristic rankings are not quantified damage estimates. []
- The FNI full article was not inspected; the publisher abstract supports only high-level claims about regional policy and its Yamal-Nenets case. []
- The 2025 Second NDC reports a second-stage adaptation plan running through 2025; post-2025 plan status and implementation were not verified. []

- War/sanctions policy context is a bounded reverse-direction observation, not a complete pathway showing interrupted adaptation delivery. []
- Regional and Arctic examples are not representative household-level vulnerability assessments; Indigenous livelihood protection appears as an objective, not delivered results. []

## Climate pressures and exposure

- Source dated 2025 reports: For two monitored sites in Nadym district, Roshydromet reports positive active-layer-thickness trends of 58 and 45 cm per decade over 2005–2024. [RUS-E-101]
- Source dated 2025-09 reports: The 2025 submission reports mean annual temperature rising 0.5°C per decade nationally since the mid-1970 s and 0.70°C per decade in the Arctic zone, 3.7 times the global rate. [RUS-E-106]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- Source dated 2025 reports: Spring-cereal growing-season precipitation in 2024 was 36% below its 1991–2020 normal in the Southern District and 30% below in the North Caucasus; local soil drought was possible in both during June–August. [RUS-E-102]
- Source dated 2025 reports: The abstract's regional ranking places heat and agricultural water stress in southwest Russia, permafrost and fire risks in the northeast, and flood risk in the southeast. Scenario: Exposure and vulnerability indicators use current conditions; hazard indicators project to mid-century under multiple SSP scenarios, whose variants are not specified in the abstract. [RUS-E-110]
- Source dated not stated reports: The Bank says some Russian regions are already experiencing more frequent droughts, floods and other hazards, while export exposure creates transition risks for companies and financial stability. [RUS-E-115]
- Source dated not stated reports: A 2023 stress test modeled falling demand for carbon-intensive products; without adaptation, mining, quarrying and metallurgy companies could see weaker financial performance in 2030–2040, creating systemic risk. Scenario: Declining demand for carbon-intensive products; the official page does not state scenario magnitudes. Estimates assume no adaptation measures. [RUS-E-116]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- Source dated 2025 reports: During active flood conditions in 2024, 2,499 operational groups were deployed more than 11,100 times, with satellite monitoring received up to seven times daily. [RUS-E-105]
- Source dated 2025-09 reports: The submission reports Moscow prioritizing electricity, heating and gas-network upgrades, alongside planting wind- and frost-resistant trees and increasing irrigation. [RUS-E-109]

## Institutions and delivery systems

- Source dated 2025 reports: EMERCOM records May 2024 natural fires over more than 44,271 ha in Yakutia; 2,728 emergency-system personnel and 187 equipment units were mobilized. [RUS-E-103]
- Source dated 2025 reports: Before the 2024 flood season, 48 federal subjects were rated ready and 37 limitedly ready; EMERCOM says the checks prompted revisions to federal and regional emergency plans. [RUS-E-104]
- Source dated 2025-09 reports: The submission says first-stage planning ended in 2022; its second-stage plan through 2025 was underway, and regional plus ten sector plans were approved. [RUS-E-107]
- Source dated 2025-09 reports: Plan tasks include reducing climate risks to the traditional livelihoods of Indigenous peoples of the North. [RUS-E-108]
- Source dated 2026-04-17 reports: The abstract says regional adaptation is often used for modernization, not led by climate policy; mitigation tasks also appear in adaptation plans. [RUS-E-111]
- Source dated 2026-04-17 reports: In Yamal-Nenets, adaptation is implemented mainly through sectoral plans embedded in infrastructure, civil protection and resource-management programmes. [RUS-E-112]
- Source dated 2026-04-17 reports: The abstract links war and sanctions to a climate-policy setting where domestic politics, not international climate diplomacy, determines Russia's response. [RUS-E-113]
- Source dated not stated reports: In its 2024 survey, most financial institutions said they factor climate risks into decisions but had not yet adopted more active risk-management approaches. [RUS-E-117]
