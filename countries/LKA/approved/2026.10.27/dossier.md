## Climate pressures and exposure

- The SCD reports 87 percent of the population lives in climate hotspots; floods and landslides mainly affect the Kelani basin, including Colombo, which accounts for about 40 percent of GDP. [LKA-E-001]
- The SCD reports recent water shortages in Northern, North-Central, and Eastern Provinces; drought also threatens hydropower, which supplies about 30 percent of electricity capacity. [LKA-E-004]
- The World Bank release reports a projected 1.2 percent annual GDP loss by 2050 due to climate change and estimates that nearly 19 million people may live in moderate or severe flood or drought hotspots by then. Scenario: The press-release summary does not specify the model scenario underlying these estimates. [LKA-E-007]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- Estimated average annual disaster fiscal losses exceed US$380 million, of which floods account for about US$240 million. [LKA-E-002]
- Poor households face disproportionate risks due to low assets, climate-sensitive livelihoods, residence near hazards, and limited safety nets and early warning. [LKA-E-003]
- The plan says women and girls are less able to adapt to climate change and more likely to face disaster-related livelihood risks and losses; it also notes gender gaps in access to land, credit, technology, information, energy, water, social protection, and employment. [LKA-E-005]
- Source dated 2025-09-30 reports: The assessment selected Mullaitivu and Batticaloa as post-conflict districts with high displacement and destruction; it describes climate-sensitive agriculture and fishing in Mullaitivu and heat, flood and drought exposure in Batticaloa. [LKA-E-101]
- UNFPA's January 2026 Ditwah report states that 165,884 displaced people were staying with hosts by 25 January, with displacement concentrated in estate-sector districts including Badulla, Kandy, Kegalle, Matale and Nuwara Eliya. [LKA-E-107]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- The implementation plan covers nine adaptation sectors and states that finances, technology, and human capital may constrain delivery of its proposed actions. [LKA-E-006]
- The 2021 program was expected to benefit about 11.2 million people through upgraded weather, flood, and landslide forecasting and early warnings; planned measures include flood-resilient infrastructure in the Kelani basin and a salinity barrier at Ambathale for Colombo's water supply. [LKA-E-008]

## Institutions and delivery systems

- Source dated 2025-10-01 reports: Eastern Province farmers reportedly perceive water and subsidies as favouring southern and central provinces; the speaker warns ignored grievances can weaken national unity. [LKA-E-105]
- Source dated 2026-01-28 reports: UNFPA and government partners audited shelters in Badulla, Kegalle, Kandy and Matale, finding WASH, privacy, security and access gaps; response included SRH/GBV services, supplies and cash. [LKA-E-109]

## Climate-FCV pathways

- Post-Ditwah shelter conditions and protection-service gaps heightened GBV risk; incidence changes were not measured. [LKA-P-101; LKA-E-108, LKA-E-109]

  - FCV mediator: Crowding, insufficient lighting and privacy, shared sanitation and gaps in accessible protection services raised exposure to harm.
  - Possible consequence: Heightened gender-based violence risk reported by UNFPA; no measured cyclone-attributable incidence.
