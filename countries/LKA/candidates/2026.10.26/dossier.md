## Climate pressures and exposure

- The SCD reports 87 percent of the population lives in climate hotspots; floods and landslides mainly affect the Kelani basin, including Colombo, which accounts for about 40 percent of GDP. [LKA-E-001]
- The SCD reports recent water shortages in Northern, North-Central, and Eastern Provinces; drought also threatens hydropower, which supplies about 30 percent of electricity capacity. [LKA-E-004]
- The World Bank release reports a projected 1.2 percent annual GDP loss by 2050 due to climate change and estimates that nearly 19 million people may live in moderate or severe flood or drought hotspots by then. [LKA-E-007]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- Estimated average annual disaster fiscal losses exceed US$380 million, of which floods account for about US$240 million. [LKA-E-002]
- Poor households face disproportionate risks due to low assets, climate-sensitive livelihoods, residence near hazards, and limited safety nets and early warning. [LKA-E-003]
- The plan says women and girls are less able to adapt to climate change and more likely to face disaster-related livelihood risks and losses; it also notes gender gaps in access to land, credit, technology, information, energy, water, social protection, and employment. [LKA-E-005]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- The implementation plan covers nine adaptation sectors and states that finances, technology, and human capital may constrain delivery of its proposed actions. [LKA-E-006]
- The 2021 program was expected to benefit about 11.2 million people through upgraded weather, flood, and landslide forecasting and early warnings; planned measures include flood-resilient infrastructure in the Kelani basin and a salinity barrier at Ambathale for Colombo's water supply. [LKA-E-008]

## Bibliography and review decision

- World Bank Group. Sri Lanka: Systematic Country Diagnostic Update. 2022-01 (date basis: publication). https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/720711651501449116/pdf/Sri-Lanka-Systematic-Country-Diagnostic-SCD-Update.pdf [LKA-SRC-001] Methodology: Diagnostic update synthesizing newer analysis, prior studies, and stakeholder consultations. Limitations: Synthesizes studies of varying dates and methods; not a new attribution study.
- Ministry of Environment, Government of Sri Lanka. Nationally Determined Contributions Implementation Plan (2021-2030). 2023-07 (date basis: publication). https://env.gov.lk/web/images/pdf/policies/Final_NDC_IP_-_10072023.pdf [LKA-SRC-002] Methodology: Government plan operationalizing sectoral mitigation and adaptation actions under the 2021 NDC through an inclusive, multi-stakeholder process. Limitations: The plan is a statement of intended actions; it notes that finances, technology, and human capital may constrain implementation.
- World Bank Group. Sri Lanka, World Bank Sign Agreement to Strengthen Climate Resilience. 2021-09-20 (date basis: portal-publication). https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2021/09/20/sri-lanka-world-bank-sign-agreement-to-strengthen-climate-climate-resilience [LKA-SRC-003] Methodology: Official World Bank release about a government program financing agreement and associated climate-risk estimates. Limitations: The page summarizes estimates without their scenario/model specifications; project benefits are expected, not realized outcomes.
