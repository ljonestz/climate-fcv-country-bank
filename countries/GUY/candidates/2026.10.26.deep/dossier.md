Critical remaining gaps:
- No countrywide causal estimate of climate impacts on conflict or violence is established by these sources. []
- Full subnational coverage and independently evaluated long-term response effectiveness remain gaps. []
- Draft collection pending human source review and approval. IDA eligibility is not an FCV classification. []
- National and selected local examples do not establish district-level coverage or causal attribution. []
- The bank contains no completed mediated climate-FCV pathway; this is an evidence gap, not evidence of absence. []
- No inspected source establishes climate as a cause of the Region 8 land disputes or quantifies their effect on climate adaptation. []
- FPP full linked PDF failed to open; claims are limited to substantive original publisher synopsis, not uninspected village tables. []
- The 2025 UN country results PDF exceeded web-reader size limits and was not used. Historical poverty/tenure findings are not current population estimates. []
- Tenure disputes and exclusion are context, not evidence of climate-caused conflict. Contemporary inclusive-recovery capacity and local effectiveness need human review. []
- Draft extension: human source review and approval remain pending. Lending eligibility is not an FCV classification. []

## Climate pressures and exposure

- Around Lethem in the Rupununi, river flooding disrupts services and livelihoods, while prolonged dry periods heighten wildfire risk. [GUY-E-002]
- Guyana's low coastal plain concentrates population and economic activity on land at or below high-tide level. [GUY-E-004]
- Source dated not stated reports: During the 2009–2010 drought, East Demerara Water Conservancy fell below the designated safe irrigation level, illustrating reservoir dependence during dry conditions. [GUY-E-105]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- Source dated 2023 reports: Rice-growing irrigation zones around Pomeroon, Demerara and Berbice depend on drainage and seawater control along the low coastal plain. [GUY-E-101]
- Source dated 2023 reports: Hinterland Amerindian communities face differentiated flood/drought vulnerability linked to poverty and location; the report separately describes mining/logging-related migration and Venezuelan arrivals in Region 1. [GUY-E-104]
- Source dated not stated reports: FAO identifies children’s water-fetching burdens and missed schooling, with women, Indigenous peoples and rural farming households among drought-vulnerable groups. [GUY-E-106]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- Lethem consultations identified Indigenous knowledge, women's preparedness training and accessible infrastructure as priorities. [GUY-E-003]
- The case study prioritizes sea defences, drainage, mangroves, disaster coordination and community organization for coastal adaptation. [GUY-E-005]
- The 2024 coastal project planned drainage-asset rehabilitation and technical capacity support for the National Drainage and Irrigation Authority. [GUY-E-007]
- Source dated not stated reports: FAO lists elevated interior child-labour vulnerability in Regions 1, 7, 8 and 9 using 2019/2020 information and describes land-title barriers limiting female-headed farms’ credit access. [GUY-E-107]

## Institutions and delivery systems

- In Georgetown, heavy rain combines with blocked drains or pump failures; limited interagency coordination complicates flood management. [GUY-E-001]
- Insufficient maintenance investment, urbanization and rising flood risk have reduced the operational capacity of Guyana's coastal drainage network. [GUY-E-006]
- Source dated 2023 reports: The 2023 assessment identifies basic agricultural recovery governance, limited sector–CDC coordination and missing actionable recovery mandates despite an enabling policy framework. [GUY-E-102]
- Source dated 2023 reports: Gender/disability inclusion lacked permanent expertise and operational actions, budgets and monitoring in agricultural recovery; policy recognition alone did not ensure implementation. [GUY-E-103]
- Source dated 2018-05-03 reports: The Region 8 tenure synopsis reports customary land excluded during titling/demarcation and resulting boundary disputes; 14 of 15 titled study villages reported no consultation or consent to granted areas. [GUY-E-108]
