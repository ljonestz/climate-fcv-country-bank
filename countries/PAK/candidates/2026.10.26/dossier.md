Critical remaining gaps:
- The 2021 NDC is a dated national synthesis; updated province-level climate and food-security indicators were not checked. []
- The PDNA is a rapid post-disaster assessment based on data collected September to mid-October 2022; it warns that inaccessible areas and time constraints limit accuracy. []
- The PDNA reports flood impacts but does not establish climate-change attribution for the 2022 event. []
- Draft collection pending human source review and approval. IDA eligibility is not an FCV classification. []
- National and selected local examples do not establish district-level coverage or causal attribution. []
- The bank contains no completed mediated climate-FCV pathway; this is an evidence gap, not evidence of absence. []

## Climate pressures and exposure

- The NDC describes GLOF risk in the upper Indus; storms and seawater intrusion on the Balochistan and Sindh coasts; river floods and agricultural heat in Punjab and Sindh; and aridity across parts of Punjab, Sindh, and Balochistan. [PAK-E-002]
- The PDNA reports 2022 floods affected 33 million people, displaced nearly 8 million, and killed over 1,700; 94 districts were calamity-hit by October 11. [PAK-E-005]
- The PDNA estimates 780,000 houses destroyed and 1.27 million partly damaged; Sindh accounted for 83% of housing damage. [PAK-E-007]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- The NDC reports Pakistan contributes about 0.9% of global GHG emissions yet is highly climate-vulnerable, citing floods, rainfall shifts, glacier melt, and vector-borne disease. [PAK-E-001]
- The NDC cites moderate or severe food insecurity among 40% of households in FY2020 as limiting capacity to absorb further climate shocks. [PAK-E-003]
- The NDC identifies agriculture as highly affected by climate variability and reports that it accounts for 95% of national freshwater withdrawals. [PAK-E-004]
- The PDNA estimates US$14.9 billion damage, US$15.2 billion losses, and US$16.3 billion recovery needs; housing, agriculture, and transport were hardest hit. [PAK-E-006]
- The PDNA estimates flood-related poverty rose 3.7–4.0 percentage points (8.4–9.1 million people) and multidimensional poverty 5.9 points. [PAK-E-008]
