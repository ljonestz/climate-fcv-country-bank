## Climate pressures and exposure

- The CCDR estimates about 463 km2 of urban area is at high risk of flooding from rivers, rain and sea-level rise; many affected urban residents live in unplanned settlements without adequate drainage. [TZA-E-001]
- The CCDR estimates 27.7 percent of Tanzania's population and 29.4 percent of its vulnerable population are exposed to at least one climate risk. [TZA-E-002]
- The NDC reports average maximum temperatures rose over 1 degree C in 1961-2013 and drying occurred in northeastern and much of southern Tanzania during 1981-2016, affecting agriculture, water and energy. [TZA-E-004]
- NDC projects 0.8-1.8 degrees C warming by the 2040s and 1.6-5.0 degrees C by 2090; dry-season rainfall may decline, wet-season rainfall rise, with southern decline and Lake Victoria-area increase by 2030. [TZA-E-005]
