## Climate pressures and exposure

- Source dated 2024-11 reports: Most urban settlements are on low-lying coastal plains; TNC names Basseterre, Sandy Point, Charlestown and Newcastle. [KNA-E-102]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- Source dated 2022-11 reports: Basseterre, home to about 27% of the population, scores medium-high on fisheries, coastal ecosystems, water and economic risks; heat and hurricanes are prominent indicators. [KNA-E-104]
- Source dated 2022-11 reports: The report states 51% of renewable freshwater was withdrawn, 31 wells supplied 70% of potable water, and 2017 drought brought severe rationing; Basseterre Valley holds the main aquifer. [KNA-E-105]
- Source dated 2022 reports: Dieppe Bay participants described homes, buildings, landing facilities and roads as exposed on a windward coast with little setback; erosion affects the settlement and coastal protection. [KNA-E-107]
- Source dated 2022 reports: Sandy Point participants linked Pump Bay erosion and past storms that damaged the cooperative and pier with difficulty hauling and securing fishing boats. [KNA-E-109]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- Source dated 2022 reports: Newcastle Bay fishers reported erosion and storm damage to the landing site and pier; the Foundation organized participation and UNDP/GEF grants supported lockers, but further facilities were requested. [KNA-E-110]
- Source dated 2026-06-18 reports: The ministry projects ten candidate wells could yield up to four million gallons daily and meet demand through 2040; drilling at Saddlers began in January 2026. Scenario: The article projects aggregate candidate-site capacity through 2040; no climate scenario or model is stated. [KNA-E-113]

## Institutions and delivery systems

- Source dated 2024-11 reports: Nevis data and downscaled-information gaps limit community-scale planning; Sandy Point and school warning systems exist, but health has none. [KNA-E-103]
- Source dated 2022-11 reports: Basseterre interviews identify difficulty reaching informal workers with policy; their limited institutional integration impedes inclusion in resilience planning and recovery. [KNA-E-106]
- Source dated 2022 reports: Hurricane Irma destroyed the Capisterre cooperative in 2017; it remained unrepaired at the 2020 assessment, leaving fishers without storage or processing space. [KNA-E-108]
- Source dated 2025-02-05 reports: At the 2025 project close, the government reported updated emergency plans, an operational and training framework for NEMA and NDMD, an impact-based multi-hazard warning framework, a storm-surge atlas and climate-data tools. [KNA-E-111]
- The June 2026 ministry update reports that Basseterre desalination began service in February, restoring round-the-clock supply for about 20,000 customers. The higher-elevation Saint Peters pipeline and pump were installed but still being tested at publication; operation there is not confirmed. [KNA-E-112]

- Stimson Center Environmental Security Program, with TaiwanICDF, Taiwan Ocean Affairs Council and Government of St. Kitts and Nevis. CORVI Risk Profile: Basseterre, St. Kitts and Nevis. 2022-11 (date basis: document-version). https://www.stimson.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Envirosec-2022-1728-CORVI-Bassaterre-NF-EDITS.pdf [KNA-SRC-102] Methodology: CORVI city profile using 96 indicators, data, 60 expert surveys, 21 interviews and desk research. Limitations: City-level empirical data were limited; survey, interview and data findings diverged. Scores are relative to regional cities.
- Caribbean Natural Resources Institute (CANARI), with the Department of Marine Resources, Government of Saint Kitts and Nevis. Report of the vulnerability and capacity assessments in coastal and fishing communities in Saint Kitts and Nevis. 2022 (date basis: publication). https://canari.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/CC4FISH-St-Kitts-Nevis-VCA-Report.pdf [KNA-SRC-103] Methodology: Three-site participatory photo-journaling, interviews and fisheries value-chain assessment. Limitations: Small purposive samples; participant findings are not representative.
- St. Kitts and Nevis Information Service (SKNIS), Government of St. Kitts and Nevis. Final Workshops Held for GCF Readiness 4 Project in St. Kitts and Nevis. 2025-02-05 (date basis: publication). https://www.sknis.gov.kn/2025/02/05/final-workshops-held-for-gcf-readiness-4-project-in-st-kitts-and-nevis/ [KNA-SRC-104] Methodology: Government news report summarizing project workshops and outputs reported by the implementing partners. Limitations: Official project summary, not an independent evaluation of whether systems are operational or outcomes sustained.

WSD Advances Water Security and Reliability Initiatives Amid Climate Change Challenges. 2026-06-18 (date basis: publication). https://mopieudt.gov.kn/wsd-achievements-2026/ [KNA-SRC-105] Methodology: Ministry news report summarizing a Water Services Department manager's June 16, 2026 progress presentation. Limitations: Government report of service and project status; future supply volumes are projections, not verified delivery outcomes.
