## Climate pressures and exposure

- Report estimates 1.5°C warming since the 1950s and rainfall decline from 85 mm (1950) to 63 mm (2020); June 2020-November 2022 drought affected up to one-third of people and worsened food insecurity. [MRT-E-001]
- The report states that floods occur nearly once every two years, have destroyed villages and livestock, and on several occasions disrupted at least 10,000 livelihoods and caused about 10 deaths. [MRT-E-002]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- World Bank modeling in the report estimates climate-related GDP losses of 9.3% by 2050 under its dry/pessimistic scenario without adaptation, compared with 5.7% with partial adaptation. [MRT-E-003]
- The G5 CCDR projects that under medium growth and its dry/pessimistic climate scenario without adaptation, Mauritania’s poverty rate in 2050 would be 0.2 percentage points above the no-climate baseline. [MRT-E-004]
