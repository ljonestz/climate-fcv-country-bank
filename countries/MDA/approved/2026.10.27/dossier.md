## Climate pressures and exposure

- Source dated 2024-02 reports: IMF estimates past climate-disaster losses near 12% of 2019 GDP cumulatively and an average 3% of the population affected by disasters over three decades. [MDA-E-106]
- Source dated not stated reports: FAO describes the south as hot and arid, with 5-6 droughts per decade; about 75% of Moldova is dry, subhumid or semiarid land. [MDA-E-108]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- Source dated 2025-05-06 reports: NDC cites a 2023 subnational index: administrative units face medium-to-very-high disaster/humanitarian risk; 28 of 34 districts have high or extreme child climate risk. [MDA-E-101]
- Source dated 2025-05-06 reports: Rural women spend 20-25 hours weekly on water and farm tasks; drought can raise workload 30%, while floods and erosion burden uninsured women farmers. [MDA-E-103]
- Source dated 2025-05-06 reports: Rural poor, Roma, migrant/refugee and disabled children are among the poorest. [MDA-E-104]
- Source dated 2024-02 reports: IMF attributes Moldova's climate sensitivity to agriculture, rural livelihoods, energy imports and limited public resources; it identifies weaker disaster preparedness, farm adaptation and infrastructure. [MDA-E-105]
- Source dated not stated reports: FAO reports rural livelihoods depend heavily on agriculture, 90% of production is rain-fed, and poor backyard producers and small farms have limited credit or subsidy access. [MDA-E-109]
- The government's October 2026 summary describes spring 2026 Dniester oil pollution following Russia's attack on Ukraine's Novodnistrovsk hydro plant. Precautionary suspension at Cosăuți affected or put about 200,000 people at risk of water disruption, with about 100,000 temporarily losing centralized supply. [MDA-E-115]
- Source dated 2026-10-16 reports: Older, disabled, low-income and transport-limited people faced added difficulty; Soroca was most affected and Bălți moved classes online. [MDA-E-116]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- Source dated not stated reports: Local action groups involve residents of eight districts, particularly women and vulnerable groups, in flood plans, dam priorities and protective measures. [MDA-E-113]

## Institutions and delivery systems

- Source dated 2024-02 reports: IMF says adaptation investment can reduce disaster losses, but domestic financing is constrained by debt and growth risks, making external concessional support and reforms important. [MDA-E-107]
- Source dated not stated reports: FAO lists a 2019 National Drought Plan, local emergency commissions' five-year preparedness plans and water-hazard warning and response protocols. [MDA-E-110]
- Source dated not stated reports: An active 2024-2027 project covers five named watersheds and targets flood early warning, dam safety and local integrated water-resources governance. [MDA-E-111]
- Source dated not stated reports: UNDP reports procurement of 10 hydrometeorological stations for five pilot areas and ground-penetrating radar for national dam monitoring. [MDA-E-112]
- Source dated not stated reports: UNDP reports training 35 specialists in digital climate-event modelling, 60 in dam flood-safety, and 50 in water-structure operation and assessment. [MDA-E-114]
- Source dated 2026-10-16 reports: Authorities, partners and businesses distributed over 620 tonnes of water; emergency medical services continued while some non-urgent care was rescheduled. [MDA-E-117]
- Source dated 2026-10-16 reports: Proposals include alternative water sources, safety plans, real-time monitoring, cross-border protocols with Ukraine, civil-protection training and support for vulnerable communities. [MDA-E-118]
