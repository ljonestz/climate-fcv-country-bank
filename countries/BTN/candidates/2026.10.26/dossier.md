## Climate pressures and exposure

- The assessment identifies glacial lake outburst floods, flash floods, landslides, forest fires, and windstorms alongside slower-onset glacial retreat and ecosystem degradation, with implications for water, food, energy, infrastructure, health, and livelihoods. [BTN-E-003]
- The report records heavy rain on 4-5 October 2025 followed by flash floods, landslides, swollen rivers, and windstorms; it notes crop, livestock, home, cultural-site, infrastructure, and hydropower impacts, one death, and two people missing, with Samtse, Chukha, Haa, and Dagana among the most affected districts. [BTN-E-004]
- The World Bank release says hotter and drier conditions could affect hydropower, tourism, and agriculture and produce economic losses by 2050; it reports that adaptation and diversification could reduce projected losses by at least 40 percent. [BTN-E-005]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- Bhutan's mountainous setting, reliance on hydropower and agriculture, and limited resources and capacity shape its climate vulnerability. [BTN-E-001]
- The release reports that about 65 percent of Bhutan's population is exposed to at least one natural hazard and identifies Zhemgang and Mongar among districts with higher risk; floods, landslides, and heat disproportionately affect vulnerable people. [BTN-E-006]

## Bibliography and review decision

- Royal Government of Bhutan. Bhutan Second Nationally Determined Contribution. 2021-06-05 (date basis: document-version). https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/NDC/2022-06/Second%20NDC%20Bhutan.pdf [BTN-SRC-001] Methodology: Government NDC describing national climate context, institutions, and planned mitigation and adaptation actions. Limitations: Policy submission; planned actions are not evaluated outcomes, and the inspected passages do not quantify local hazard exposure.
- United Nations Development Programme Bhutan. Assessment of Climate-Induced Loss and Damage in Bhutan. 2026-02-06 (date basis: portal-publication). https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp-bhutan-assessment_of_climate_induced_loss_and_damage_report.pdf [BTN-SRC-002] Methodology: Review of secondary documents, key-informant group interviews, stakeholder workshop, and field visits. Limitations: The report notes limited primary datasets and dispersed institutional knowledge; the inspected 2025 event passage reports impacts but does not attribute the event to climate change.
- World Bank Group. Adaptation, Climate Resilience, and Diversification Key to Creating Jobs, Accelerating Growth in Bhutan. 2025-06-18 (date basis: portal-publication). https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/06/18/adaptation-climate-resilience-and-diversification-key-to-creating-jobs-accelerating-growth-in-bhutan [BTN-SRC-003] Methodology: Official World Bank release summarizing findings and recommendations from its Bhutan Country Climate and Development Report. Limitations: The press-release summary does not provide underlying model specifications; the qualitative hotter-and-drier scenario is not assigned a named pathway.
