## Climate pressures and exposure

- The communication reports that more than 80 percent of Kenya is arid or semi-arid; typical annual rainfall is 200-500 mm in arid areas and 600-1,000 mm in semi-arid areas, and is erratic. [KEN-E-001]
- The NDC reports that the 2022-2023 drought caused losses exceeding KES 70 billion and pushed about 300,000 additional people into poverty. [KEN-E-004]
- The NDC projects mean surface temperature to rise about 1.0-1.7 degrees C by 2035, with larger warming in lowlands; rainfall projections vary by season and county under RCP2.6 and RCP8.5. Scenario: RCP2.6 and RCP8.5, as identified for rainfall projections in the NDC [KEN-E-006]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- The NDC reports that floods in 2024 affected 412,763 people, including 107,318 women of reproductive age and 9,300 pregnant women whose health services were disrupted. [KEN-E-005]
- Turkana's May 2023 county assessment describes drought, floods, intense rainfall and unreliable seasons affecting pastoral, farming, fishing and water-dependent livelihoods. [KEN-E-101]
- The May 2023 Turkana assessment describes women and girls traveling far for riverbed water and higher drought-related malnutrition risks for pregnant/lactating women, children and older people. [KEN-E-103]
- The 2023 review describes historical wind-park job disputes between Turkana and Samburu in Sarima and a failed borehole desalination unit; these illustrate project-benefit and service risks, not proof that climate hazards caused violence. [KEN-E-109]
- At publication in 2023, the review described oil-site roadblocks and attacks over water and employment, alongside prospective production risks; this is not an update on 2026 oil operations. [KEN-E-110]

## Resilience and peace-supporting capacities

- The communication states that drought-related scarcity of water and pasture contributes to resource competition and intercommunity conflict in arid counties. [KEN-E-002]
- Turkana's May 2023 county assessment describes drought and heat reducing pasture and water and increasing grazing competition and the likelihood of insecurity. [KEN-E-102]
- International Alert's February 2026 publisher summary describes drought, floods and resource pressures amplifying competition among pastoral, agropastoral and fishing communities in Marsabit and Turkana. [KEN-E-105]
- International Alert's February 2026 summary describes unequal land/water access, movement and conservation restrictions as livelihood and tension mediators, and recommends inclusive conflict-sensitive programming. [KEN-E-106]
- A 2023 structured review describes drought-related pasture and water stress alongside resource-related raids in Turkana, Samburu and Marsabit, while emphasizing contested and varying mechanisms. [KEN-E-107]
- Saferworld reports supporting a Samburu rangeland-access legislative process during 2018-2022, alongside a peace/cohesion forum; institutional outputs do not establish reduced conflict. [KEN-E-112]
- ACIAR's July 2023 publisher summary recommends recognizing local pastoral-service networks in Isiolo and Marsabit, including herders, herd owners, animal-health providers, mobile-money services and brokers. [KEN-E-113]
