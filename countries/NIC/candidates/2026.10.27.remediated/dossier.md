# Nicaragua Climate-FCV Evidence Dossier

## Executive assessment

- The Dry Corridor evidence links erratic rain and production stress with food and schooling coping, without municipal incidence estimates. [NIC-E-101, NIC-E-102]
- Bilwi/Waspam information channels and Alal conflict displacement add local FCV context; neither establishes that climate caused armed violence or that radio closure measurably reduced adaptation. [NIC-E-104, NIC-E-105]
- SINAREM launch and WFP plan priorities are institutional/design evidence, not verified performance. [NIC-E-106, NIC-E-107]

## Climate pressures and exposure

- WFP identifies droughts, hurricanes, storms and floods as threats to Nicaragua's food systems. [NIC-E-005]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- Eta and Iota hit Nicaragua's Caribbean autonomous regions, where extreme poverty and Indigenous and Afro-descendant populations increased vulnerability to shocks. [NIC-E-001]
- Small farmers and vulnerable households in Nicaragua's Dry Corridor face heightened food-security risks from climate extremes. [NIC-E-006]
- Source dated 2026-04-23 reports: IACHR identifies Nicaragua’s Dry Corridor as 63 municipalities (37% of territory), reliant partly on subsistence/small-scale farming; 2024 El Niño intensified drought and reduced rain, harming crop output and food security. [NIC-E-101]
- Source dated 2026-04-23 reports: In the Dry Corridor, below-average and erratic rain reduced production and deepened household food insecurity; vulnerable families reduced food quantity or quality and withdrew children from school. [NIC-E-102]
- Source dated 2025-08-25 reports: IACHR reports an armed-settler attack on Mayangna Alal (Sauni As, 30 km from Bonanza, Bosawás) in January 2020: at least four killed, over 16 homes burned, survival goods looted and 79 families displaced. [NIC-E-105]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- The 2021 response prioritized restoring services, livelihoods and small-scale fisheries with affected communities involved in choosing activities. [NIC-E-002]
- Source dated 2026-09 reports: WFP’s 2024–2029 plan commits to integrated disaster-risk management, nutrition-sensitive social protection and school feeding linked to smallholder resilience, especially for women and climate-adaptive farming capacities. [NIC-E-107]

## Institutions and delivery systems

- In Nueva Segovia, Madriz and Estelí, the evaluation found improved producer and cooperative capacity, with stronger early resilience gains among participating women. [NIC-E-003]
- Shared resilience systems were only partially institutionalized; sustained support was needed to consolidate achievements. [NIC-E-004]
- Source dated 2026-04-23 reports: IACHR reports Nicaragua’s 2025 legal changes weakened environmental safeguards and participation or consultation mechanisms in a context of high climate vulnerability. [NIC-E-103]
- IACHR's 2025 report documents the September 2023 closure of Bilwi/Waspam Indigenous radios serving 17 territories, including channels for reporting violence and climate/environment concerns. [NIC-E-104]
- Source dated 2025-02-21 reports: SINAPRED announced SINAREM’s launch on 20 February 2025; the system is designed for real-time monitoring, early warning and response to disasters including floods. [NIC-E-106]
