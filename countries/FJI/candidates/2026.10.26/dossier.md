# Fiji Climate-FCV Evidence Dossier

## Executive assessment

- The CCDR summary identifies stronger cyclones, heat, drought, floods and rising seas as threats to Fiji's infrastructure, agriculture and tourism. [FJI-E-001]
- The report recommends upgrading vulnerable informal settlements and targeting shock-responsive social protection, rather than treating household exposure as uniform. [FJI-E-002]
- Government coordination, implementation monitoring and budgeting need strengthening; the summary describes existing sovereign disaster financing as covering relatively modest events. [FJI-E-003]

## Climate pressures and exposure

- The CCDR summary identifies stronger cyclones, heat, drought, floods and rising seas as threats to Fiji's infrastructure, agriculture and tourism. [FJI-E-001]
- UNDP identifies rural, maritime and coastal communities with greater exposure and less access to timely warnings. [FJI-E-004]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- The report recommends upgrading vulnerable informal settlements and targeting shock-responsive social protection, rather than treating household exposure as uniform. [FJI-E-002]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- Warning barriers include connectivity, language, mobility and accessibility constraints affecting women, older people, persons with disabilities and marginalized communities. [FJI-E-005]
- The initiative proposes inclusive warning design and coordinated institutions; its launch does not demonstrate reliable last-mile delivery. [FJI-E-006]

## Institutions and delivery systems

- Government coordination, implementation monitoring and budgeting need strengthening; the summary describes existing sovereign disaster financing as covering relatively modest events. [FJI-E-003]
