## Climate pressures and exposure

- Castries and Anse La Raye face flood risks linked to steep terrain, coastal settlement and exposed infrastructure. [LCA-E-001]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- Saint Lucia's coastal concentration exposes people and economic activity to storms, flooding and drought while placing pressure on public finances. [LCA-E-003]
- The Saint Lucia analysis links unequal climate vulnerability to poverty, food insecurity and access to essential services, with disproportionate risks for women. [LCA-E-005]
- Source dated 2013 reports: The Castries assessment identifies Black Mallet, Maynard Hill, Entrepot, Bocage and Pavee in Castries East, Bois Patat and Morne Dudon in Castries North, and La Pansee in Castries Central as poor, unplanned hillside settlements exposed to landslides. [LCA-E-101]
- The March 2014 assessment reports that the December 2013 trough heavily affected Anse La Raye, Canaries, Soufriere, Micoud, Choiseul and Vieux Fort. More than 550 people were evacuated to shelters or other refuges, with poverty affecting vulnerability. [LCA-E-103]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- The2025 urban flood project planned drainage, river restoration, early warning and community coordination in Castries and Anse La Raye. [LCA-E-002]
- The programme proposes gender-responsive vulnerability analysis and integrated social protection linked to disaster preparedness. [LCA-E-006]
- Source dated 2026-05-01 reports: The proposed regional programme targets ages 10-29 with public-health and socio-ecological prevention, youth participation and gender-responsive interventions; in May 2026 stakeholders were still validating its design and governance arrangements. [LCA-E-107]

## Institutions and delivery systems

- Saint Lucia adopted a disaster-risk financing strategy and public-asset management measures to strengthen preparedness. [LCA-E-004]
- Source dated 2013 reports: The assessment describes Castries risk governance as reactive, with risk identification, reduction and adaptive governance weak and most investment focused on emergency response and short-term recovery. [LCA-E-102]
- The 2013 trough damaged roads, bridges, John Compton Dam's water system and distribution pipes; some communities lacked piped water for up to ten days. [LCA-E-104]
- Source dated 2022 reports: UNDRR's 2022 summary says Saint Lucia's DRR framework includes NEMO, a multi-sectoral advisory council and a Hazard Mitigation Council; it also flags limited disaggregated data and lists civil unrest among the country's hazards. [LCA-E-105]
