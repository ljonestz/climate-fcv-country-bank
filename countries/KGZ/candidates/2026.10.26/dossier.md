## Climate pressures and exposure

- The World Bank summary reports more severe storms, landslides, heat waves, and droughts; inaction could cut GDP 2–3% by 2040 via agriculture and infrastructure damage. [KGZ-E-004]
- The summary estimates up to 24% of irrigation demand could go unmet by mid-century as temperatures and crop-water needs rise. [KGZ-E-006]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- The 2021 NDC describes the Kyrgyz Republic as highly climate-vulnerable and reliant on climate-sensitive sectors; disasters, disease, infrastructure damage, and reduced water can threaten development, energy, and irrigation. [KGZ-E-001]
- The NDC says women and children in rural households carry much of the work of obtaining domestic water and fuel, while women face barriers to finance, training, technology, and climate decision-making. [KGZ-E-002]
- The summary says inaction could push 170,000 more people into poverty by 2040, especially in rural areas, and reduce jobs and incomes for the bottom 20%. [KGZ-E-005]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- The Resilient Landscape Restoration Project aims to benefit over 50,000 rural people, including women and vulnerable groups, across Jalal-Abad, Osh, Issyk-Kul, and Naryn regions. [KGZ-E-007]
- The project plans to improve glacier, snow-cover, and mudflow monitoring and restore landscapes with local labor payments and livelihood training; implementation is planned through 2029. [KGZ-E-008]
