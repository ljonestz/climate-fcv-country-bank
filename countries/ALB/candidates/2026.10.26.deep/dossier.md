# Albania Climate-FCV Evidence Dossier

## Executive assessment

- Municipal examples distinguish socioeconomic vulnerability from modeled flood exposure; informal riverbank communities face additional housing and coping constraints. [ALB-E-101, ALB-E-102, ALB-E-103, ALB-E-105, ALB-E-106, ALB-E-107]
- Social protection cannot yet fully scale for climate shocks. Local plans and ALBAdapt monitoring/early-warning ambitions add institutional detail but do not prove delivered risk reduction. [ALB-E-104, ALB-E-108, ALB-E-111, ALB-E-112, ALB-E-113, ALB-E-114]

Critical remaining gaps:
- No pre-existing ALB country dossier was present under countries/ in this worktree; this is new-country Phase 2 extraction. []
- The National Security Strategy's climate-security passage is global risk framing, not an Albania-specific causal finding; no armed-conflict or climate-related violence link was established. []
- The English Ministry-hosted National Security Strategy file returned HTTP 502; the official Albanian PDF was opened and translated. The full Shkodër local adaptation plan was not linked, only its project summary. []
- No accessible source linked conflict or insecurity to weaker climate adaptation or disaster-service delivery; this is an evidence gap, not evidence of absence. []
- The global climate-security strategy passage is excluded from substantive Albania evidence. No complete Albania-specific climate–violence or reverse insecurity chain is supported. []
- Municipal vulnerability scores reflect population change and market access; flood rankings use a modeled event. Neither is a count of people already harmed. []
- Draft extension: human source review and approval remain pending. Lending eligibility is not an FCV classification. []

## Climate pressures and exposure

- Source dated 2024-10 reports: The CCDR's ranked high-flood-exposure list names Fier, Lushnje, Shkodër, Roskovec, Divjakë, Rrogozhinë and Vau i Dejës. [ALB-E-102]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- Source dated 2024-10 reports: The CCDR's socioeconomic-vulnerability list includes Dropull, Këlcyrë, Përmet, Kolonjë, Fushë Arrës and Mallakastër among the highest-ranked municipalities. [ALB-E-101]
- Source dated 2024-10 reports: Flooding has worsened exposure for disadvantaged informal communities along riverbeds in Tirana, Durrës, Vlora, Shkodër and Lezha. [ALB-E-103]
- Source dated 2025-10 reports: NAP profiles flag hotter, drier summers in Durrës; erosion, landslides and shortages in Elbasan; floods across Fier; and flood/intense-rain risk in Kukës. [ALB-E-105]
- Source dated 2025-10 reports: Future urban risk is rated very high for Tirana, Durrës, Vlorë, Fier, Shkodër, Elbasan and the coastal belt across four subsectors. [ALB-E-106]
- Source dated 2025-10 reports: Marginalized riverbank neighborhoods face heat and heavy-rain risk; women are more vulnerable. Without climate-linked social protection, risks may trigger community tipping points. [ALB-E-107]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- Source dated 2024-10 reports: The CCDR says low coverage and benefit adequacy limit climate-shock support; Ndihma Ekonomike systems showed flexibility during the 2019 earthquake response but cannot yet scale to additional climate-affected poor households. [ALB-E-104]
- Source dated 2026-07-13 reports: ALBAdapt plans to modernize climate and meteorological monitoring, forecasting and digital climate data to improve services for agriculture, tourism and civil protection. [ALB-E-112]
- Source dated 2026-07-13 reports: The programme is establishing a people-centred multi-hazard early-warning system to improve interagency coordination; about 700,000 people are expected to benefit directly. [ALB-E-113]
- Source dated 2026-07-13 reports: ALBAdapt supports planned nature-based measures, local preparedness and risk-sensitive planning in the most climate-exposed coastal areas. [ALB-E-114]

## Institutions and delivery systems

- Source dated 2025-10 reports: The NAP reports eight municipal plans developed with technical staff, data collection and capacity-building; each identified about 85 adaptation measures and 45 priorities on average. [ALB-E-108]
- Source dated not stated reports: ETMI reports Shkodër's local adaptation plan was delivered in 2025, with sector analyses, prioritized actions and a governance and monitoring framework supported by stakeholder engagement. [ALB-E-111]
