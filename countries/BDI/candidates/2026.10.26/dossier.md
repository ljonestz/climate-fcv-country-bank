## Climate pressures and exposure

- The report projects 0.5–1.0°C warming for 2040–2060 versus 1981–2000, with uncertain north–south rainfall differences. [BDI-E-001]
- Prior national analysis projected 1–3°C higher annual mean temperature and roughly 10% more rainfall by 2050, with a disrupted seasonal regime. [BDI-E-004]
- The INDC lists groundwater shortage, lowland flooding, soil-fertility loss, crop-season changes and unpredictable pest movements among projected risks. [BDI-E-006]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- The composite assessment scores 347 of 2,616 collines at very high combined climate, environmental and fragility risk, and none at low or very low risk. [BDI-E-002]
- The INDC identifies northern and northwestern vulnerability hotspots and flags the slope toward the Imbo Plain, northern terrain and central plateau as erosion-prone. [BDI-E-005]
