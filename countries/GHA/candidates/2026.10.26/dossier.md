# Ghana Climate-FCV Evidence Dossier

## Executive assessment

- The CCDR reports about 45,000 people affected by floods annually and identifies roughly half of Ghana’s 550-km coastline as vulnerable to erosion and flooding associated with sea-level rise. [GHA-E-002]
- CCDR modelling estimates climate change could raise Ghana’s poverty rate by 1–2 percentage points by 2050, or leave about one million more people poor than a no-climate-change case. [GHA-E-001]
- Ghana’s updated NDC sets out 19 policy actions, including 13 adaptation actions, and estimates planned coverage of 38 million people by 2030, mainly women and youth; EPA and NDPC have coordination and planning roles. [GHA-E-006]

## Climate pressures and exposure

- The CCDR reports about 45,000 people affected by floods annually and identifies roughly half of Ghana’s 550-km coastline as vulnerable to erosion and flooding associated with sea-level rise. [GHA-E-002]
- The brief says a three-month 2024 drought cut yields by about half in affected locations; recurring Bagre Dam floods have displaced communities and damaged farms, homes, and roads. [GHA-E-004]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- CCDR modelling estimates climate change could raise Ghana’s poverty rate by 1–2 percentage points by 2050, or leave about one million more people poor than a no-climate-change case. [GHA-E-001]
- Across the study area, respondents most often identified drought (39%), bushfires (22%), and floods (16%) as climate-related concerns; more than 97% of surveyed households engaged in crop farming. [GHA-E-003]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

Climate-Proofing Northern Ghana’s Future: Investing in Resilient Livelihoods and Infrastructure. 2025-11 (date basis: document-version). https://soco.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/20251125_Climate-Resiliency-in-Northern-Ghana-Policy-Brief.pdf [GHA-SRC-002] Methodology: Policy brief reports a mixed-method study with geospatial analysis and interviews involving 210 participants.
