## Climate pressures and exposure

- WFP consulted Trio communities around Kwamalasamutu and Saamaka communities along the Upper Suriname river on livelihood and supply-chain risks. [SUR-E-004]
- Source dated 2023-06 reports: Heavy rains in March 2022 flooded Brokopondo and other interior districts; reported effects included crop losses, hunger and nearly 1,500 displaced people, while flooded southern roads challenged relief access. [SUR-E-105]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- Low river levels during drought restrict boat access to remote Indigenous and Maroon villages, raising transport costs and disrupting essential supplies. [SUR-E-003]
- Source dated not stated reports: IICA’s 2025 project listing says remote Maroon farms face crop-production losses from increasingly frequent extreme rainfall and drought, alongside constrained tools, resources and technical knowledge. [SUR-E-110]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- The 2025 project planned community-led resilience activities in Paramaribo, Saramacca and Wanica, including women-led initiatives. [SUR-E-002]
- Paramaribo's waterfront adaptation project links flood protection with heritage, transport access and livelihoods around the Central Market. [SUR-E-006]
- Source dated 2025-04-01 reports: In 2022, EnGenDER provided 95 water tanks to three Indigenous and 34 tribal communities in Brokopondo and Sipaliwini districts; Klaaskreek also received water-system rehabilitation. [SUR-E-102]
- Source dated 2025-04-01 reports: An IICA project installed low-cost rainwater systems at selected households in Matta and Singri Lanti (Para District) and Tamarin (Marowijne District) to expand potable-water storage. [SUR-E-103]
- Source dated 2025-04-01 reports: The report says schools near riverbeds in Boven-Suriname and Marowijne are considered for relocation from flood-prone sites, but greater distance may impede pupil and teacher access. [SUR-E-104]
- Source dated 2023-06 reports: In the 2023 phone survey, 61% of households reported extreme rainfall as a threat, 41% reported flood threat, and 57% were unprepared for disasters; unpreparedness was 64% for female-headed versus 59% for male-headed households. [SUR-E-106]
- Source dated not stated reports: IICA reports that micro-sprinkler plots improved farmers’ use and scheduling of irrigation water, reducing water loss in its interior farming project. [SUR-E-108]
- Source dated not stated reports: IICA says a crop nursery contributed to recovering bitter cassava and brown upland-rice varieties that had been lost following drought and flooding. [SUR-E-109]
- Source dated not stated reports: The project planned farmer training and two micro-irrigated multi-crop plots plus a nursery to strengthen Maroon farmers’ climate-smart production skills. [SUR-E-111]

## Institutions and delivery systems
