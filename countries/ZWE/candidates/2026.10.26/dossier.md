## Climate pressures and exposure

- NDC names droughts, floods and heatwaves as major disasters, lists drought seasons in 2012-13, 2015-16, 2018-19 and 2023-24, and says drought exposure varies across Zimbabwe. [ZWE-E-001]
- NDC locates flood exposure in low-lying areas, dam overflows, river confluences, upstream backwater and cyclone paths; floods have spread to previously unaffected areas. [ZWE-E-002]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- World Bank says agriculture employs 70 percent of Zimbabwe's population; reliance on rainfed systems and maize leaves the sector vulnerable to climate events, especially ENSO-linked droughts. [ZWE-E-004]
- World Bank attributes the 2023-24 severe drought to El Nino and reports maize yield 60 percent below the five-year average, alongside widespread food insecurity and economic hardship. [ZWE-E-005]
- World Bank reports that the 2023-24 El Nino drought caused about US$363 million in damage and losses. [ZWE-E-006]
