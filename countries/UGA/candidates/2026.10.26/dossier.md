## Climate pressures and exposure

- The CCDR reports heavy rainfall, floods and droughts affected about 200,000 Ugandans per year on average over the prior two decades. [UGA-E-001]
- Climate-related internal migration could reach 12 million by 2050, mainly in the Lake Victoria Basin. [UGA-E-002]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- The CCDR identifies northern, northeastern and north-central households as highly exposed to weather shocks with inadequate coping strategies. [UGA-E-004]
- The NDC risk assessment rates crop-and-livestock vulnerability at 4.07 and risk at 4.18; forestry vulnerability is 3.25 and risk 4.25. [UGA-E-005]

## Coping and adaptive capacity
