## Climate pressures and exposure

- NDC reports floods, droughts and cyclones have doubled in frequency since the 1980s. [MWI-E-002]
- The NDC reports Cyclones Idai (2019), Ana (2022) and Freddy (2023) caused widespread infrastructure, crop and livestock damage and displaced over one million people. [MWI-E-003]
- Without climate-resilience investments, the CCDR projects GDP reductions of 3-9 percent by 2030, 6-20 percent by 2040 and 8-16 percent by 2050. [MWI-E-005]
- The CCDR summary says climate change could push another two million people into poverty over the following decade. [MWI-E-006]
