Critical remaining gaps:
- The NDC's 2026 figures are summarized in French, and the opened Grand Sud CCDR passage does not isolate climate's contribution to violence; no country-specific causal estimate of climate effects on conflict was opened. []
- Draft collection pending human source review and approval. IDA eligibility is not an FCV classification. []
- National and selected local examples do not establish district-level coverage or causal attribution. []
- The bank contains no completed mediated climate-FCV pathway; this is an evidence gap, not evidence of absence. []

- A disaster displacement count is exposure evidence, without violent-conflict or anthropogenic event attribution. [MDG-E-006]

## Climate pressures and exposure

- The CCDR links Grand Sud drought and cyclone flooding with crop/livelihood losses, high prices and food insecurity; over two million faced acute hunger in the 2020-2022 crisis, also involving COVID price shocks. [MDG-E-001]
- NDC reports Grand Sud droughts affected over 1.3 million people in 2015-2022; in 2024 over 1.2 million faced acute food crisis. [MDG-E-004]
- The NDC estimates that 57 percent of schools have high cyclone exposure and 50 percent have high drought exposure. [MDG-E-005]
- The NDC reports 285,800 people were forcibly displaced in 2022 following sudden climate-related events, including tropical cyclones and floods. [MDG-E-006]
