## Climate pressures and exposure

- The project targets flood-related disruption to roads, bridges, markets and essential services on Tongatapu. [TON-E-003]
- Source dated 2025 reports: The government review reports current coastal flooding in low-lying Popua and Sopu; Sopu wetlands buffer high tides by about 30 cm, but the protection is weakening as seas rise. [TON-E-101]
- Source dated 2025 reports: A 2025 review summarizes an ADB (2019) projection that by about 2090 high tides may inundate low-lying Nuku'alofa, possibly forcing residential-area abandonment. Scenario: Scenario is unspecified in the ADB (2019) summary. [TON-E-102]
- The TNA cites 2019 government information identifying drought vulnerability nationally, with higher proportions in Tongatapu and Vava'u. [TON-E-105]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- Source dated 2023-12 reports: The assessment identifies drought and possible groundwater salinization on low-lying Ha'apai islands; some remote Ha'apai and Vava'u islands rely solely on rainwater and face rainfall variation. [TON-E-104]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- The announcement describes cyclone disruption alongside high fuel costs and a narrow tax base as pressures on public and household finances. [TON-E-001]
- Reforms enable mobile-phone disaster alerts, including for remote outer islands, with intended benefits for women, children and isolated communities. [TON-E-002]
- Planned resilient transport links and drainage aim to maintain connectivity and evacuation access; benefits depend on delivery. [TON-E-004]
- Source dated 2025 reports: Tonga's 2020-2023 One Million Trees initiative planted mostly mangroves; the review says it may have strengthened coastal resilience, but land saved is unquantified and Sopu's wetland buffer may be shrinking. [TON-E-103]
- Source dated not stated reports: Live & Learn describes a 2021-2026 programme across 15 communities in Ha'apai (4), Vava'u (8) and 'Eua (3), targeting community plans, nature-based actions, training for 20 representatives and direct actions for 20 households per site. [TON-E-108]

## Institutions and delivery systems

- The operation provides contingent crisis financing for weather and other emergencies; financial availability is not evidence of effective household assistance. [TON-E-005]
- The announcement records Cabinet approval of a national social protection policy intended to improve disaster assistance to vulnerable citizens. [TON-E-006]
- Source dated 2023-12 reports: Village water committees manage rural and outer-island supplies; the assessment says community systems are more weather-exposed than utility systems and need support to prepare for drought. [TON-E-106]
- Source dated 2024-05-13 reports: Stakeholders identified Tonga's water sector as highly vulnerable and proposed better groundwater monitoring, village water-committee training and wider use of spatial data in planning and vulnerability assessment. [TON-E-107]
- Source dated 2024-01-15 reports: During the 2022 volcanic tsunami, communities used radio, word-of-mouth and inherited hazard knowledge to move people inland or to high ground; UNDRR reports four deaths despite widespread impact. [TON-E-109]
