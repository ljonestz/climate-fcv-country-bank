## Climate pressures and exposure

- Communication reports retreat along 40 km of sandy coast at an average 10 m yearly, aggravated by storms and local sediment dynamics. [TGO-E-002]
- Under RCP6.0, sea level is projected to rise 11.35 cm by 2025 and 62 cm by 2100, worsening coastal erosion and livelihood risks. [TGO-E-003]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- Agriculture vulnerability is high nationwide and very high in Savanes; under RCP6.0, Kara also reaches very high by 2050. [TGO-E-001]
- NDC reports 2020-2021 Oti and Mono floods caused deaths and damaged roads, markets, health centers, homes, schools, power and telecoms. [TGO-E-006]
- NDC estimates 6,902 hectares of food crops flooded in 2020 and production losses near 9,000 tonnes; dry spells also reduced yields. [TGO-E-007]
