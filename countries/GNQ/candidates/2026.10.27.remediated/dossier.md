## Climate pressures and exposure

- Source dated 2022-06 reports: The NDC relies on only two ASECNA stations, in Malabo and Bata; cited trends show significantly rising temperatures in Malabo but a different pattern in Bata, with no significant accumulated-rainfall trend. [GNQ-E-103]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- Source dated 2022-06 reports: Citing the 2013 adaptation plan, the NDC reports that residents had noted more storms and floods, drying springs and warmer conditions; it labels the country highly vulnerable. [GNQ-E-104]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- Source dated 2024-06 reports: The World Bank reports that no national forestry inventory has been completed since 1992 and no forestry management plans have been formulated or implemented; the governing law also awaits update. [GNQ-E-107]

## Institutions and delivery systems

- The 2019 communication anticipates climate pressure on water and describes competing control/use among water managers, recommending basin coordination. The passage concerns administrative resource governance, not documented armed conflict. Scenario: The communication does not specify a climate scenario. [GNQ-E-101]
- Source dated 2019 reports: The report says the absence of a national meteorological system prevents services for agriculture, fishing and water management, including early warnings for floods and dangerous weather. [GNQ-E-102]
- Source dated 2022-06 reports: The 2022 NDC sets targets for protecting 50% of the coast by 2050 and restoring 1,300 hectares and conserving 24,700 hectares of mangroves. [GNQ-E-105]
- Source dated not stated reports: The government requested SCALA support to strengthen private-sector participation in adaptation; follow-up discussions sought evidence for climate-resilient agriculture and value-chain work linked to the GCF program. [GNQ-E-106]
- The 2024 economic update identifies inadequate support for communal forests, indigenous populations, rural women and smallholders; participatory management and benefit-sharing are recommendations, not delivered peacebuilding capacity. [GNQ-E-108]
