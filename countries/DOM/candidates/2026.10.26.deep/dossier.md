## Climate pressures and exposure

- Source dated 2023-04 reports: The note cites estimates that informal settlements house 43% of Greater Santo Domingo residents and 70% of Puerto Plata residents. [DOM-E-104]
- Source dated 2023-04 reports: Surveyed household-goods losses associated with floods or excess rainfall in 2019–21 were concentrated mainly in the south and southwest. [DOM-E-105]
- Source dated 2025-11 reports: IMF reports mean temperature rose 0.73°C since 1950; another 0.63–1.94°C rise and declining precipitation are projected by 2050. [DOM-E-109]
- Source dated 2025-11 reports: Hurricane Fiona (2022) damaged over 8,500 homes, displaced over 43,000 people and disrupted water for more than one million households. [DOM-E-110]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- Source dated 2024 reports: Interviewees in Restauración, Dajabón, linked rainfall shifts and land degradation to lower farm yields and migration intentions. [DOM-E-102]
- Source dated 2023-04 reports: Poor people were 46% of residents in highly flood-prone areas; one in four households in the bottom two wealth quintiles faced high flood risk. [DOM-E-106]
- Source dated 2023-04 reports: Female-headed households were less likely than male-headed households to receive flood warnings (40% vs 47%) or seek weather information (50% vs 56%). [DOM-E-108]
- Source dated 2025-11 reports: Six in ten residents experience intermittent water supply; low-income households may spend up to 12% of income on bottled water. [DOM-E-111]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- Source dated 2024 reports: Some Restauración farmers diversified into forestry and agroforestry; local association nurseries provided plants, but time, land and investment constrained poorer farmers. [DOM-E-103]
- Source dated 2023-04 reports: The note reports poor households may sell assets, migrate, remove children from school, or cut food, health and education consumption after climate shocks. [DOM-E-107]

## Institutions and delivery systems

- Source dated 2025-11 reports: IVACC and FIBE guide Emergency Bonus targeting using structural damage, health effects and income loss after weather emergencies. [DOM-E-112]
- Source dated 2025-11 reports: Municipal disaster committees prepare local emergency plans, but misaligned plans and weak cross-level coordination hinder unified response. [DOM-E-113]

## Climate-FCV pathways

- Projected mobility may expose gaps in border services and resource access; tension is conditional, not a migrant threat attribution. [DOM-P-101; DOM-E-101]
  - Documented impact: Modeled internal and Haiti-origin migration into urban, farming and border areas.
  - FCV mediator: Under-served border infrastructure and unequal access to limited resources.
  - Possible consequence: Possible host–migrant tensions, not observed violence or a claim that migrants inherently create insecurity.

  - Uncertainty: Conditional modeled pathway, not an observed 2026 forecast or causal violence estimate; border-policy assumptions may not hold.
