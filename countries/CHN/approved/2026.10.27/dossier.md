## Climate pressures and exposure

- Source dated 2025-06 reports: MEE reports 2024 national precipitation 9% above the 1991–2020 norm; Yangtze-Huaihe Meiyu rainfall exceeded normal by over 50%, and North China rainy-season precipitation by 83%. [CHN-E-105]
- Source dated 2025-01-17 reports: Local emergency departments reported 94.13 million person-times affected by disasters in 2024, 856 deaths or missing, and 3.645 million emergency relocations nationwide. [CHN-E-109]
- The 2024 disaster bulletin reports national flood/geological-disaster person-time impacts and emergency relocations, with deadly July events in Shaanxi's Shangluo/Baoji and Hunan's Chenzhou/Hengyang. Administrative counts are not unique-person prevalence or climate-attributable losses. [CHN-E-110]
- Source dated 2025-01-17 reports: Super Typhoon Yagi affected Hainan, Guangdong, Guangxi and Yunnan in September 2024, causing four deaths and 189,000 collapsed or damaged houses, with reported direct losses of 72.03 billion yuan. [CHN-E-111]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- Source dated 2018-12 reports: The communication identifies the Loess Plateau as China's most agriculturally vulnerable area; observed yield reductions affected one crop type on 90% and two types on 55% of its arable land. [CHN-E-101]
- Source dated 2018-12 reports: Warming and drying reduced northern-grassland forage yield and capacity, most in central-eastern Inner Mongolia and southeastern Gansu. [CHN-E-102]
- Source dated 2018-12 reports: By mid-century, water vulnerability is projected to intensify in Haihe, Yellow, Huaihe and Song-Liao basins under combined climate change and rising demand. Scenario: Medium greenhouse-gas concentration scenario; comparison period 2021–2050 versus 1961–1990. [CHN-E-103]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- The ministry's 2024 progress report describes Qinghai-Tibetan Plateau coordination and research and reports 333.8 hectares of additional greening around Yellow River-area rivers, canals, lakes and reservoirs. These are government-reported activities, not independently evaluated resilience outcomes. [CHN-E-107]

## Institutions and delivery systems

- Source dated 2025-06 reports: In 2024, China added 9,944 geological-hazard warning stations; six administrative levels logged 600,000 risk reports and helped move over 10,000 households and 30,000 people before danger. [CHN-E-106]
- The June 2025 ministry report records adaptation plans in 30 provinces and the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps by February 2025, and a 2024 list of 39 climate-adaptive city pilots. Counts do not establish implementation quality or reduced losses. [CHN-E-108]
- Source dated 2025-06-06 reports: In Tunshang Village, Zima Township, Qinglong County, Guizhou, warning and joint inspections led to 180 people moving from a landslide-risk area; 53 houses were damaged, with no deaths. [CHN-E-112]
- The ministry's selected Yangjiawan/Huangzhou case describes detection of levee seepage and emergency control works. Its estimates of people and farmland protected are reported counterfactual exposure estimates, not independently measured avoided losses. [CHN-E-113]
- Source dated 2025-06-06 reports: Before August 2024 storms, Pinggu District responders checked households including older, infirm, disabled and non-local residents in Tawa Village; 257 people were relocated from flood and landslide risk. [CHN-E-114]
