## Climate pressures and exposure

- Source dated 2025 reports: 2022-23 deficits reached 81% in unnamed basins and cereal output fell 67%; the NDC records 50.4°C heat in Agadir and over 6,000 hectares burned in Oriental in 2023. [MAR-E-101]
- The NDC reports historical flood deaths at Ourika in 1995 and Guelmim in 2014, and a separate national total of nearly 22,000 internal displacements in 2008-2024. That displacement total is not assigned to these cities or particular flood events. [MAR-E-102]
- Source dated 2022-01 reports: The plan reports 1960-2018 rainfall decline, warming and increased aridity; southern and southeast Atlas provinces are arid, while Souss, El Haouz and Oriental include semi-arid zones. [MAR-E-106]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- The NDC reports drought-related fruit losses in Gharb, Haouz and Saïss in 2022 and disproportionate livelihood pressure on rural women. Its discussion links farm precarity with rural out-migration; it does not establish violence or allocate migration counts to these regions. [MAR-E-103]
- Source dated 2022-01 reports: By 2050, catch potential could fall 5% under RCP8.5; artisanal fishers, small aquaculture producers and women-majority fish processors are identified as especially exposed. Scenario: RCP8.5 projection to 2050. [MAR-E-107]
- Source dated 2020 reports: In modeled case SC4, a 25% water-supply reduction plus climate-related yield changes reduces national unskilled-labor demand by 7%; permanent losses may expand urban migration. Scenario: SC4: 25% water-supply reduction combined with crop-yield shocks; report describes medium- to long-run effects. [MAR-E-109]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- Source dated 2025 reports: Regional plans in Oriental, Drâa-Tafilalet, Souss-Massa, Béni Mellal-Khénifra and Marrakech-Safi target oasis and rural adaptation. [MAR-E-104]
- Morocco's NDC proposes wastewater reuse, groundwater management and interbasin transfers in 2026-2035 as measures intended to reduce water-stress migration. Benefits are not measured; interbasin transfer is not cross-border migration. Scenario: Proposed 2026-2035 measures; no modeled scenario. [MAR-E-105]
- Source dated 2022-01 reports: The plan proposes oasis livelihood diversification and climate early warning, and community-led vulnerability mapping and water options tailored to each mountain massif. [MAR-E-108]
- Source dated 2020 reports: The 2020 report says the government program supports sprinkler and drip irrigation, targeting 555,000 hectares and a reduction in flood-irrigated land from 81% to 43%. [MAR-E-110]
- Source dated 2026-02-05 reports: The report records 5,651 activity participants, 45% women and 52% youth, against design targets of 30% and 40%; another section gives a different reach total. [MAR-E-112]

## Institutions and delivery systems

- Source dated 2026-02-05 reports: The 2025 mission found cumulative delays, 9% effective physical execution and under 1% disbursement; it attributed slow progress partly to limited provincial agriculture-department capacity and delayed technical assistance. [MAR-E-111]
- Source dated 2026-02-05 reports: Strong recent floods damaged the provisionally accepted Amejjaou track in Driouch; missing drainage and slope protections leave its durability at risk under intensifying extreme rainfall. [MAR-E-113]
