## Climate pressures and exposure

- Remote outer islands have limited government services and climate-adapted infrastructure; their access constraints differ from densely populated South Tarawa. [KIR-E-001]
- Low atoll terrain, drought and groundwater salinization threaten drinking water and agriculture. [KIR-E-004]
- Kiribati's draft June 2025 report of the 2023-2024 household survey estimates about one quarter of households affected by natural disasters, most often strong winds; no island-level breakdown is established. [KIR-E-101]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- The draft-marked March 2025 NC3 describes historical severe freshwater shortage and well salinity in South Tarawa and Banaba during droughts; it separately mentions temporary abandonment of unspecified islands. [KIR-E-103]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- Planned investments combine freshwater, drainage, coastal protection and public-building maintenance with island-council participation and universal access. [KIR-E-002]
- SOFF support aims to strengthen weather observations and forecasting capacity; anticipated benefits are not demonstrated household outcomes. [KIR-E-006]
- UNDP lists an ongoing whole-of-islands programme targeting five pilot islands and 17,500 people (49% women), with planned island-level climate-resilient planning and water, food-security and infrastructure measures. [KIR-E-106]
- UNDRR's 2025 publisher summary describes satellite connectivity and drought anticipatory action as early-warning approaches for remote islands; it does not measure island coverage or effectiveness. [KIR-E-108]

## Institutions and delivery systems

- The announcement reports improved water sources under the preceding adaptation phase, without an independent evaluation of sustained service quality. [KIR-E-003]
- The feature identifies observation infrastructure, financing and reliable local climate information as constraints on anticipatory decisions. [KIR-E-005]
- The draft-marked March 2025 NC3 describes water-reserve declarations on private land generating tensions, disputes and vandalism; encroachment is separately noted in South Tarawa, Banaba and Tabakea. [KIR-E-105]
- UNDP identifies institutional and technical barriers to adaptation and lists stronger policies, island-level planning, knowledge and communication as programme components. [KIR-E-107]
