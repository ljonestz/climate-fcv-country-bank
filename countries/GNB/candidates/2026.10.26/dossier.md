## Climate pressures and exposure

- The NDC says coastal and marine ecosystems face storms, flooding, erosion and sea-level rise, with growing freshwater scarcity and declining marine productivity. [GNB-E-001]
- The NDC reports mangroves cover about 22% of the country and declined approximately 19% in 2001–2021; they support fisheries and many livelihoods. [GNB-E-002]
- NC4 reports coastal displacement from land and housing loss, farmer crop failures amid unpredictable rainfall, and threats to Bijagós ecosystems. [GNB-E-005]
- For 2016–2045, the NDC projects coastal warming of 1.2°C under RCP4.5 and 1.3°C under RCP8.5, versus 1.4°C and 1.5°C inland; rainfall is projected to rise slightly or stagnate, with local exceptions. [GNB-E-007]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- NC4 identifies vulnerabilities in coastal, Bijagós, inland agricultural, and riverside areas, linked to poverty, natural-resource dependence, and institutional and infrastructure weaknesses. [GNB-E-004]
- The NDC describes the interior as prone to longer droughts, fires, floods, and water shortages; the coastal zone holds about 70% of the population and faces saline intrusion in agricultural fields. [GNB-E-008]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- The NDC proposes 50 km of coastal and riverine anti-salt dykes by 2035, prioritizing high-risk areas with predominantly women residents; mangrove restoration is also targeted. [GNB-E-003]

## Institutions and delivery systems

- NC4 identifies weak cross-sector coordination, insufficient data and climate finance, and a lack of compensation or insurance mechanisms for climate-related loss and damage. [GNB-E-006]
