## Differentiated vulnerability

- The summary says unplanned urbanization heightens city climate risk; more frequent, intense floods could cause losses and damage critical infrastructure. [LAO-E-001]
- Climate change is increasing water-resource pressure, and hydropower is likely to be significantly affected. [LAO-E-002]
- The CCDR summary says climate change could harm rice cultivation and livestock, disproportionately affecting poor people and increasing farmers’ need for adaptation support. [LAO-E-003]
- The NDC reports 2018 floods affected more than 600,000 people nationwide, with damage and losses estimated at US$371 million, or 2% of GDP. [LAO-E-005]
- The NDC says tropical storms and depressions in 2019 affected more than 764,000 people across two central and four southern provinces, disrupting livelihoods and damaging property. [LAO-E-006]
- The NDC expects warming across the Mekong region but says rainfall changes vary by location; altered temperature and precipitation may worsen flood conditions and affect crops, hydropower, roads, water supply, and food security. [LAO-E-007]
