## Climate pressures and exposure

- The CCDR projects that by 2070, 98% of Benin’s territory could face extreme temperatures under a 2.7°C global-warming pathway associated with current policies. Scenario: 2.7°C global warming under current policies, as stated in the CCDR passage. [BEN-E-004]
- The World Bank summary says average annual GDP losses from climate change could reach up to 19% by 2050 without additional adaptation. Scenario: Without additional adaptation; the inspected summary does not specify the underlying climate scenario. [BEN-E-005]
- The NDC identifies 30 communes in the coastal zone, five on the seafront, and reports shoreline retreat of 12–30 metres per year at critical points alongside flood, drought, wind and sea-level risks. [BEN-E-007]
- Southern floods along the Ouémé River and low-lying coast damage crops, displace communities and disrupt infrastructure and market access. [BEN-E-103]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- In EHCVM 2018/19, 21.4% of households reported a climate-induced shock during the previous three years; 10.5% reported flood impacts and 12% drought impacts. [BEN-E-002]
- Poor, rural and agriculture-reliant households reported greater exposure to climate shocks; over 36% of agriculture-reliant households reported flood and/or drought impacts, versus about 21% overall. [BEN-E-003]
- The NDC reports that agriculture employs about 70% of the population and accounts for 72.9% of export receipts; it identifies rainfall extremes, floods, dry spells, heat and strong winds as risks and lists 30 sectoral plus 51 commune-led adaptation projects. [BEN-E-006]
- Southern Benin has two rainy seasons and diversified livelihoods; central and northern areas have one rainy season and mixed farming or agropastoralism. [BEN-E-101]
- Northern drought and dry spells reduce crop yields; pastoralists face degraded pasture and dry-season water scarcity. [BEN-E-102]

## Coping and adaptive capacity

- Insecurity in Alibori and Atacora restricts mobility, farm access and markets while driving repeated displacement. [BEN-E-104]
- FEWS NET's June 2026 context report describes northern farmer-pastoralist conflicts damaging crops and livestock and restricting farming and pastoral mobility. [BEN-E-105]
- The February 2025 appraisal describes attacks in Matéri and Kérou in Atakora, and Karimama and Banikoara in Alibori, causing school closures and southward movements that pressure host communities. [BEN-E-107]

## Institutions and delivery systems

- The February 2025 project document proposes climate-screened water/services infrastructure, livelihood support and participatory planning for refugee and host communities in targeted northern areas. [BEN-E-108]
