## Climate pressures and exposure

- SCD reports recurrent floods, landslides, erosion and storms threaten lives, livelihoods, the airport and primary roads concentrated along the coast. [STP-E-005]
- World Bank climate data show 1951-2010 warming of 1.5°C, mean rainfall falling 1.7 mm yearly, and March-May rainfall dropping 10.5 mm monthly. [STP-E-006]

## Differentiated vulnerability

- NDC reports December 2021-February 2022 floods and landslides damaged infrastructure, agriculture and WASH; recovery cost $37.5m, about 7% of 2022 GDP. [STP-E-001]
- NDC cites annual flood losses rising from 1.9% of GDP in 2020 to 2.8% by 2050 and 4.1% by 2080. [STP-E-002]
- NDC states fisheries provide 70-85% of animal protein and support 15% of active workers; extreme weather and erosion damage boats and isolate communities. [STP-E-003]
